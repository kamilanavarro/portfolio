Case study

Save Sight Institute

At the Save Sight Institute, I work across research communications to connect eye research with the people it matters to — from researchers and patients to supporters and the wider public.

My work spans digital communications, science storytelling and events, translating research and institutional priorities into content and experiences that are clear, engaging and meaningful for different audiences.

My approach

The audience changes. The research doesn't have to get lost.

Good research communication isn't simply about making information shorter or simpler. I think about who needs to understand the work, what matters to them and how the story can meet them there while staying true to the research.
