About

Science has always been where I start.

I'm a science editor, communications specialist and community builder working at the intersection of science, people and storytelling.

My story

I started in science. Then I found my way into telling its stories.

Trained as a molecular biologist, I moved from the lab into science communication, working across editorial, digital content and community building in Asia and Australia.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of science, people and communication — whether I'm shaping a research story, building a community around science or creating conversations that make specialist ideas accessible.
