Featured community

Pinoy Scientists

Pinoy Scientists is a digital platform that showcases the lives, work and experiences of Filipino scientists through social media takeovers and community-led science communication.

As Managing Director for Communications, I help shape how the platform tells stories, engages its audience and creates space for Filipino scientists to connect with one another and with the wider public.

My role

Building connection, not just reach.

My work focuses on the communications that keep a community active: shaping stories, creating accessible digital content and finding ways for scientists and audiences to participate rather than simply observe.

At its core, Pinoy Scientists is about visibility and connection — giving Filipino scientists a platform to share who they are, what they do and the many different paths a life in science can take.
