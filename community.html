My role

I joined Pinoy Scientists as co-founder in 2018, introducing the now-signature weekly takeover format to put Filipino scientists in charge of telling their own stories. What started as a new approach to the platform has since grown into a thriving community of over 84,000 people.

As Managing Director for Communications, I oversee the platform’s communications strategy, coordinating weekly takeovers with Filipino scientists around the world and serving as the first point of contact for contributors throughout the process. I also lead our communications team, introduced our student internships and have managed interns and campus ambassadors.

I also help guide how Pinoy Scientists grows and evolves. Over the years, this has meant reimagining how we reach and engage our audience — shaping campaigns, events and community programmes, building relationships with stakeholders across the Philippine scientific ecosystem, and launching new platforms including our website and newsletter. Along the way, we’ve worked with organisations including the Philippine Department of Science and Technology, Philippine Space Agency, Philippine-American Academy of Science and Engineering, British Council Philippines and GradMAP.

Digital platform

Pinoy Scientists Website

I proposed a dedicated website to give our featured scientists’ stories a life beyond their weekly social media takeovers. I also initiated a broader rebrand of Pinoy Scientists, which laid the foundation for a new website built around that identity. I shaped the site’s information architecture and led its content development, writing the copy across the website. Today, it is the first — and so far only — searchable resource for Filipino researchers worldwide, regularly used by students, media and fellow scientists to find relevant expertise.

Community event

P.S. Pathways to Science

As the Pinoy Scientists community grew, I saw an opportunity to create new ways for our featured scientists and audiences to connect. P.S. Pathways to Science was our first event, showcasing the diverse career paths of our featured scientists. I helped shape the programme and recruited speakers, moderating a panel focused on less conventional careers in STEM. Since then, Pinoy Scientists has expanded into in-person events, including regular meetups and hands-on bootcamps in areas such as data visualisation and bioinformatics.

Community publication

The Pinoy Scientists Newsletter

I launched the monthly Pinoy Scientists newsletter to bring together science news, job opportunities, conferences and events relevant to Filipinos, keeping our wider community informed and connected. It also gave us a new way to repurpose our growing archive, revisiting past takeovers and keeping our featured scientists and their work in the conversation. I built the newsletter from the ground up, defining its format and editorial direction. After producing the early issues myself, I eventually handed production to my team. The newsletter has since grown to more than 400 subscribers.
